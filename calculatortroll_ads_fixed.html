How to use this online calculator

This calculator is designed for quick arithmetic. Select the number buttons and operation buttons, then press = to calculate. Press C to clear the display and start again.

➕ Addition

Add two or more numbers together.

Example: 12 + 8 = 20

➖ Subtraction

Find the difference between numbers.

Example: 15 − 6 = 9

✖️ Multiplication

Multiply one number by another.

Example: 7 × 4 = 28

➗ Division

Divide a number into equal parts.

Example: 20 ÷ 5 = 4

Worked examples

Example 1: 18 + 24
Enter 18, press +, enter 24, and press =. The result is 42.

Example 2: 9 × 6
Enter 9, press ×, enter 6, and press =. The result is 54.

Example 3: 30 ÷ 3
Enter 30, press ÷, enter 3, and press =. The result is 10.

Normal mode and Choose Answer mode

Normal: calculates and displays the result. Choose Answer: turns the result into a small multiple-choice challenge. Pick the answer you think is correct. This is a casual practice feature, not a formal assessment.
